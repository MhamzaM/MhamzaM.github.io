Privacy policy: Tasbeeh

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Tasbeeh is a free dhikr counter made by MhamzaM (FreeApps by Hamza).

Summary

The app does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Details

  • No internet access. The Android app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
  • Saved on your device only. Your counts, history, custom adhkar and settings are stored on your phone (or in your browser when you use the website) and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Vibration. The only permission the app uses is vibration, for feedback while counting.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
