Privacy policy: TV Remote

Last updated: 24 November 2026

TV Remote is a free, open-source remote control app for Google TV, Android TV and Roku TV, made by MhamzaM (FreeApps by Hamza). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Summary

The app does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app does on your phone

  • Finds TVs on your network. The app sends discovery messages (mDNS and SSDP) on your Wi-Fi network to find compatible TVs. This traffic stays on your local network.
  • Talks directly to your TV. Button presses, text you type with the app's keyboard and app-launch commands go straight from your phone to your TV over your local network. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Remembers your TVs. The names and local IP addresses of TVs you add are saved on your phone.
  • Pairing certificate. To pair with a Google TV or Android TV, the app creates a security certificate that is stored only on your phone and shared only with TVs you pair with.

All of this stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Network access: to communicate with TVs on your local network.
  • Wi-Fi state and multicast: to discover TVs on your Wi-Fi network.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
