Privacy policy: Masjid Times

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Masjid Times is a free app by MhamzaM (FreeApps by Hamza) that shows the jamaat times of nearby masjids, as reported and confirmed by the people who pray there. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Account. You don't sign up. The first time you use the app it gets a random anonymous ID from our server (Supabase), so your confirmations can be counted once. It isn't linked to your name, phone number, email or contacts.

Location.

  • Finding nearby masjids: your location is sent to the server to list the masjids around you. It isn't stored.
  • Contributing (updating a time, confirming, adding a masjid): your location is sent so the server can check you're at the masjid and that the phone isn't using a fake-location app. Only the distance from you to the masjid is stored, never your coordinates.
  • Adding a masjid: your location at that moment becomes the masjid's location on the map.
  • The map is drawn with tiles from OpenStreetMap's servers, which receive the map area you're viewing (like any website showing a map). See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Location is used only while the app is open. The app never tracks you in the background.

What is stored on the server: masjid names and locations; jamaat times; proposed changes with their agree/disagree counts; for each contribution, your anonymous ID, the time and your distance from the masjid. These records are used only to judge which times are correct and to limit abuse, for example by spotting someone who keeps submitting wrong times. Other users never see your ID.

On your phone: your chosen masjid, the masjids you follow, reminder settings and a saved copy of their times. Every few hours the app downloads the latest times for the masjids you follow, so it can update your reminders and tell you if a time changed. That download sends only the masjids' IDs.

Credits: map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); maps drawn with Leaflet (BSD-2-Clause).

Permissions: internet; location (only while the app is open); notifications; exact alarms (so reminders are on time); starting after reboot (to restore reminders).

Deleting your data: uninstalling the app removes everything on your phone. Contributions stay on the server under the anonymous ID, which can't be traced back to you. To have them removed, open an issue with the approximate date and masjid.

Questions: https://github.com/MhamzaM/masjid-times/issues
