Privacy policy: Firefly Choir

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Firefly Choir is a free game made by MhamzaM (FreeApps by Hamza).

Summary

The game does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Details

  • No internet access. The Android app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
  • Saved on your device only. Your best score and the sound setting are stored on your phone (or in your browser when you play on the website) and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Vibration. The only permission the app uses is vibration, for a short buzz during play.

Children

The game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
